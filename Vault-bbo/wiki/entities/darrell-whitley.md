Darrell Whitley is a genetic-algorithm researcher at Colorado State University. He authored the widely cited *A Genetic Algorithm Tutorial* ([[whitley-1994-ga-tutorial]]) and developed the **Genitor** steady-state GA and the **CHC** algorithm. A long-serving figure in the evolutionary-computation community (editor-in-chief of *Evolutionary Computation*).
