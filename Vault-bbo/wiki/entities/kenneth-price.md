Kenneth Price co-invented [[differential-evolution]] (DE) with [[rainer-storn]]. He proposed the original difference-vector mutation idea and the `DE/best/2/bin` variant, and co-authored the foundational 1997 paper [[storn-price-1997-differential-evolution]] and the later book *Differential Evolution: A Practical Approach to Global Optimization* (2005).
