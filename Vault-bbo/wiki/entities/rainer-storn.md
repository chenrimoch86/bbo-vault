Rainer Storn co-invented [[differential-evolution]] (DE) with [[kenneth-price]], introduced in their 1997 paper [[storn-price-1997-differential-evolution]]. At the time he was at Siemens AG (Munich). He maintains the long-running DE homepage and reference C code, and contributed to popularizing DE as a simple, robust continuous global optimizer.
