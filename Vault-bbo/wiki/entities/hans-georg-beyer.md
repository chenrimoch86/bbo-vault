Hans-Georg Beyer is a leading theorist of [[evolution-strategies]], known for rigorous analysis of ES dynamics, convergence, and the role of recombination (the "genetic repair" principle). Author of *The Theory of Evolution Strategies* (2001) and co-author, with [[hans-paul-schwefel]], of the comprehensive ES survey [[beyer-schwefel-2002-evolution-strategies]].
