Hans-Paul Schwefel is a co-founder of [[evolution-strategies]] (ES), which he developed with Ingo Rechenberg as students at TU Berlin in the 1960s. His work introduced **comma (μ,λ) and plus (μ+λ) multimembered selection** and the **self-adaptation of mutation strengths**, and his book *Evolution and Optimum Seeking* (1995) is a standard reference. He later led the computer-science group at the University of Dortmund. Co-author of the comprehensive ES survey [[beyer-schwefel-2002-evolution-strategies]].
