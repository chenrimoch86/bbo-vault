# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974). Director of the US Office of Scientific Research and Development during WWII.

## Relevance to this wiki

Author of "As We May Think" (1945), in which he described the **[[Memex]]** — a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents. The essay is widely cited as a conceptual ancestor of hypertext and the web.

The [[LLM Wiki — The Core Idea]] pattern is spiritually closest to Bush's vision: private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves. The problem Bush couldn't solve was maintenance cost. LLMs solve that.

## Key work

- **"As We May Think"** (1945, *The Atlantic*) — describes the Memex concept.
