# Memex

A hypothetical personal knowledge device described by [[Vannevar Bush]] in "As We May Think" (1945). The name is a portmanteau of *memory* and *index*.

## What it was

A desk-sized device that would store all of a person's books, records, and communications, and allow rapid retrieval via associative trails — links between documents that the user creates and follows. Bush's key insight: the human mind works by association, not by index. The Memex would mirror that.

## Why it matters here

The [[LLM Wiki — The Core Idea]] is the closest practical realization of the Memex vision: a private, actively curated knowledge store where the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves. The piece Bush couldn't solve was *who maintains the trails*. In the LLM Wiki, the LLM does.
